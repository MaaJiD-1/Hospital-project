Welcome City Hospital Multan

Since Its opening, City Hospital. has been expanding Its services and equipment to meet the growing healthcare needs of the human community. The objectives of City Hospital are to provide tertiary level patient care and serve as a referral hospital also to conduct teaching/ training of doctors and other health workers at various levels in the field of medicine and surgery.

Providing Best Medical Care

Our diverse platform knows how to provide quality care to our patients and families. Our best medical care involves accurate response time, hospital atmosphere, and doctor’s manner. These all factors are considered well in our services and how people feel about the quality of our services. Our medical care also refers to clinical data collection expertise and analysis of this data over some time. Different groups and agencies are involved in different ways for reporting various clinical outcomes
